## Loading screen: bar overflow fixed + shine sweep (user request, 2026-10-30 ~20:45)

- **Report:** "when loading, the loading bar goes out the screen, fix that, and … use shiny text or sum shi to make it all nice n good".
- **Cause of the overflow.** The old draw path derived the wordmark height from `min(width * 0.155, height * 0.33)` and then placed the bar at `logoY + logoHeight * 0.86 + 28` in raw pixels. On short or portrait windows (e.g. 480×1080, 100×80) the texture box plus that fixed 28 px offset ran past the bottom edge, and the bar could also exceed the window width because it was a fixed fraction of the logo. The texture's transparent padding (text occupies x 273–1790, y 107–406 of 2048×512) made the *drawn* rect and the *visible* text disagree, so a bar "below the logo" was never actually below the text.
- **`gui/JilatonLoadingScene` (new, 153 lines).** All geometry now lives in one pure `layout(width, height)` that returns a `Layout` record (logo box + bar box, plus `textTop()`/`textBottom()` derived from the texture's visible fractions). It caps the logo by **both** a width cap (`(w - 2·margin)/4`) and a height cap (`(h - 2·margin)/0.86`), centres a text+gap+bar block on 45 % of the window height, and clamps logo, bar x and bar y inside the window; bar width is `min(max(72, logoWidth·0.55), w - 2·margin)`. The bar can no longer leave the screen at any resolution.
- **Shine.** `render` draws the backdrop, the wordmark at 90 % of the fade alpha with a violet-white tint (`0xFAF8FF`), then three stacked sweep layers (soft halo half-width 0.36 / 0.22 peak, mid sweep 0.15 / 0.50, sharp glint 0.055 / 1.0) as 32 sub-rectangle blits of the wordmark texture with per-strip alpha, so light glides across the letters every 2.6 s and rests off-screen between passes. The band is pure (`shine(time, fraction)`), so its shape is unit-tested rather than eyeballed.
- **Progress bar.** Rounded track (`JilatonUi.fillRounded`, 16 % alpha), accent-violet fill (`0xFF8878FF` mixed 22 % toward white), a travelling glint clipped to the filled part, a soft under-fill line, and a bright leading-edge cap that only draws while progress is incomplete.
