### Shared rotation handling

`dev.jilaton.client.rotation` now arbitrates one-tick visible-rotation requests by explicit module priority, wraps yaw over the shortest path, clamps pitch, and limits turn speed. AimAssist, BowAim, KillAura, AnchorAura, and AutoRod submit through it; AutoPot suspends requests while its throw angle is active. Pure policy and lifecycle tests cover priority, tie stability, wraparound, step bounds, invalid inputs, and per-tick suspension. This is an adapted visible-camera subset of LiquidBounce's REA/CFR-inspected rotation infrastructure; it does not implement silent/server rotations, movement correction, or live mouse/render reconciliation. It has compiled and passed unit tests but has not been validated in Minecraft.
